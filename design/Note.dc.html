The plan that never left the notebook

A beautifully designed plan that never leaves the notebook is, technically speaking, stationery.

Body copy sets here, one column, at a comfortable measure. Paragraphs stay short. The essay carries its own rhythm and needs no decoration beyond the occasional rule.

A second paragraph continues the thought. Where a point deserves emphasis it takes italics, never bold, and where a section ends a hairline rule marks the break.

A subheading, set in the display face

Sections are separated by a rule and a subheading. Pull quotes sit full width, outside this column, in the italic cut.

Most stuck decisions are two decisions wearing one coat.

The essay closes without a summary. The final paragraph is the point, not a recap of it.
