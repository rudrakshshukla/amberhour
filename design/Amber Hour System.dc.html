Think before you build.
Assumptions I made
  1. Editorial-studio voice from your brief: a quiet magazine about thinking, not a template marketplace.
  2. The Library is a curated collection; counsel sits above it. Placeholders stand in until the tools exist.
  3. Your brief asks for warm off-white, near-black and one restrained accent — green is that accent, and also the ink. Cream reverses on green; espresso holds photographic warmth.
  4. PP Editorial Old (Ultralight, Regular, Italics) is loaded from your files, and the marks are your real logo artwork, masked so each lockup can take green or cream.

Green ramp 100 → 900. Steps 100–200 for tinted hovers and table stripes, 500 for small labels and meta, 700 for the ink itself. Sage stays on rules and larger type — at 10.5px it only reaches 3.3:1 on the cream ground, so labels take accent-500 instead.
